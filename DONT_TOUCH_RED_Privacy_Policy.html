DON'T TOUCH RED – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game DON'T TOUCH RED handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of DON'T TOUCH RED is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. The game does not intentionally request or collect your contacts, SMS messages, precise location, camera, or microphone.

The game may store gameplay progress locally on your device, such as your best score, highest stage, gameplay statistics, settings, and achievement/progression information. This local information is used to provide the game's core features and is not, by itself, a request for your identity.

2. Personal Information

We do not intentionally require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, home address, or government identification in order to play the core game.

3. Advertising and Sponsorship

The game may support advertising or clearly identified sponsor placements in future versions. If third-party advertising or analytics services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to identify the relevant services and explain the categories of information they may process.

Advertisements or sponsor placements are intended to remain separate from active gameplay controls and should not be designed to mislead players.

4. Analytics

The current game build does not intentionally send the locally stored gameplay data described above to us as a personal profile. If analytics services are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated before or when those services are used.

5. Internet and Offline Use

The core gameplay is designed to work offline where technically possible. Some future features, such as online leaderboards or other network-dependent services, may require an internet connection.

6. Children's Privacy

DON'T TOUCH RED is a general casual game. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided to us through the game or in connection with the game, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

If future versions use third-party services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, online leaderboards, or other network services, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies. The DON'T TOUCH RED Privacy Policy will be updated to identify material third-party data practices that apply to the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game's data practices or features change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
